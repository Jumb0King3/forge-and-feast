# Forge and Feast 2.4.1

Crucible shows in the Kiln Oven model (Iron and Steel)

  - A crucible in a firing slot now shows in the model, in the same place a
    clay pot or mould would sit.
  - It glows dimly while its ingredients are melting, and glows full-bright
    orange once the metal is molten.
  - Molten metal left in the kiln stays hot while the kiln is hot. The glow
    turns off and the model redraws when you take it out or it cools.
