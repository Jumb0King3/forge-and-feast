# Forge and Feast 2.2.0

New: Charcoal Retort

  - Turns firewood and logs into charcoal. Firewood takes 2 per batch, logs 1.
    Needs about 250 C to work.
  - Tiers: copper, bronze, iron, steel (maximum temperature 400 / 450 / 500 /
    550 C).
  - Connects to the same chimneys and stovepipes as the other stoves.
  - Makes much more smoke than other stoves when used indoors (2.5x).
  - Recipes: copper from copper plates and burned bricks; upgrade to the next
    tier with plates.
