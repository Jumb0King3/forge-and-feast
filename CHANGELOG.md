# Forge and Feast 1.3.0

Smoke, chimneys and stovepipes

  - Smoke fills a closed room if you light a stove without a chimney
    (choking damage after about 15 seconds).
  - Clay Brick Chimney on top gives a draft: no smoke and faster cooking.
  - Stovepipes added (craft: 2 metal plates in a column = 6 pipes). They join
    up with bends automatically and carry smoke from a stove to a chimney or
    out through a wall. Pipes do not join to a firepit.
  - Range models: copper shows 2 burner plates, bronze 3, iron 4.
