FORGE AND FEAST - CHANGELOG AND VERSION LIST
============================================
Mod id: forgeandfeast    Server: The Peeps    Game: Vintage Story 1.22.7
Every version is kept on S:\ as forgeandfeast_<version>.zip. Newest first.

NOTE ON 1.x ENTRIES: the notes for 2.0.0 and later were written as each
version was released. The notes for 1.0.0 to 1.7.1 were rebuilt afterwards by
comparing what changed inside each zip, so they describe what the files show
(and the original test list) rather than a release note written at the time.


2.14.1  - Primitive Survival: its smoker is switched off
  Only applies when Primitive Survival is installed next to Forge and Feast.
  - The Primitive Survival smoker can no longer be crafted, and neither can
    its trussed meat (only the smoker used it). The Forge and Feast Smoker
    does the job instead, and now handles crab and snake meat too.
  - Nothing already in a world is removed: smokers already placed keep working,
    and smoked meat, cured smoked meat and jerky are left alone.
  - New file: patches/primitivesurvival-no-smoker.json.

2.14.0  - Primitive Survival: cured crab and snake meat
  Everything here only switches on when Primitive Survival is installed.
  Primitive Survival is not on The Peeps right now, so nothing changes in the
  game until it is added. Not yet tested in game.
  - Crab meat and snake meat now have a cured form. Put raw crab or snake meat
    in the Smoker and it comes out as cured meat (same keeping time as the base
    game's cured meat: very long). The cured meat can be cooked in soups and
    meaty stews in the pot, like the other cured meats.
  - Cured nuggets: chop cured crab or snake meat with a knife or cleaver to get
    cured crab or snake nuggets. You can also cure raw nuggets in the Smoker
    straight away, the same as the meat nuggets.
  - Recipes that take raw crab or snake nuggets (pemmican, dumplings, breaded
    balls, stuffed peppers, acorn versions, crab and snake pemmican) also take
    the cured ones, the same way they already take cured meat nuggets.
    Sausages stay raw only, the same as meat sausages.
  - Kitchen Range: crab meat, snake meat, smoked meat and cured smoked meat
    (Primitive Survival) can go in the oven or on a burner, like other meat.
  - Primitive Survival has its own smoker (trussed meat into smoked meat) and
    jerky. They are left alone and work as before next to the Forge and Feast
    Smoker.
  - New files: patches/primitivesurvival-cured.json,
    patches/primitivesurvival-cured-recipes.json.

2.13.0  - Kitchen Range: foods go where they would in real life
  - Every food now goes in the oven slots, the burners, or either, by what
    it is. Baked and roasted foods (bread, dough, muffins, hardtack, pemmican,
    dried fruit, stuffed peppers, roasted nuts and the like) are oven only.
    Fried, sauteed and boiled foods (breaded nuggets and vegetables, sausages,
    dumplings, nuggets, mushrooms, pickled vegetables, grain) are burner only.
    Raw meat, poultry, fish, vegetables and roots can go in either. The full
    list is in range_oven_vs_burner_review.xlsx.
  - Burners now cook food that has baking steps the way a pan would, through
    the same steps as the oven (raw, part-cooked, cooked) at a steady pace, and
    the finished food moves to the output slot before it can char. Before, raw
    meat on a burner went straight to charred meat (Expanded Foods changes it so).
  - Fixes 2.12.4, which let dough and part-baked bread sit on a burner.
  - Feverstone Wilds bison leg, ribs and cockatrice tail can now be roasted
    in the oven (raw, part-cooked, cooked), as well as cooked on a burner.
  - A pot holding a single raw meat that can also be baked no longer chars it
    on its own.
  - Food not in the list follows what it can do: bake only goes in the oven,
    cook only goes on a burner, both goes in either. Pots and other non-food
    items are unchanged.
  - New files: src/RangeStations.cs and patches/feverstone-baking.json.

2.12.4  - Kitchen Range: items that both bake and cook
  - Burners now take items that have a plain cooked or baked form even if they
    can also be baked in an oven (for example the Expanded Foods breaded and
    acorn items, which have both). Before, the range sent every bakeable item
    to the oven slots only and refused it in a burner, which a firepit and the
    Potbelly Stove do not. Pure oven items (bread dough and the like) still go to
    the oven slots only.
  - Crucibles and other metal-smelting containers are still refused by the
    range on purpose. The Kiln Oven handles those.

2.12.3  - Smoked Feverstone Wilds meats work in recipes
  - The smoked bison leg, ribs and cockatrice tail are now accepted everywhere
    the base game's cured red meat or cured poultry is accepted by Expanded
    Foods and the Expanded Foods Meals Module:
      Breaded meat nuggets (and the acorn version), pasta (hot and cold),
      rice and beans, au gratin, salad, and porridge.
    Bison leg and ribs count as red meat, cockatrice tail as poultry.
  - They are only added when Feverstone Wilds and that mod are both installed.
  - Not changed: Feverstone's raw and cooked meats are still not accepted by
    these meals (Feverstone never added them), and I could not check the base
    game's own meal recipes (soup, stew, pie and so on), because their files are
    not available here. If a pot refuses a smoked Feverstone meat, send the log.
  - New file: assets/forgeandfeast/patches/feverstone-smoked-recipes.json.

2.12.2  - New: smoked Feverstone Wilds meats
  - Added real smoked versions of the three Feverstone Wilds meats that had
    none: Smoked bison leg, Smoked ribs and Smoked cockatrice tail. They use
    the Feverstone models with a new smoky brown texture, and show up in the
    handbook and creative menu next to the raw, cooked and charred versions.
  - The smoker now turns the raw bison leg, ribs and cockatrice tail into
    these, one for one. (2.12.1 made them cured red meat and cured poultry.)
  - Smoked versions keep like the base game's cured meat (about a year fresh).
    Satiety: bison leg 300, ribs 250, cockatrice tail 250 (cooked is 400, 350
    and 350). They add nothing and change nothing if Feverstone Wilds is not
    installed. If the new items fail to load, the smoker falls back to the
    2.12.1 cured meat.
  - New file: assets/forgeandfeast/patches/feverstone-smoked.json and
    textures/item/smokedmeat.png.

2.12.1  - Smoker: Feverstone Wilds meats
  - Checked the smoker against the meats from every mod on the server.
  - Works already: raw red meat, bushmeat and poultry (including everything the
    Legacy of the Phanerozoic dinosaurs drop) become the base game's cured meat,
    and raw fish fillets become smoked fish. Expanded Foods meat nuggets cure too.
  - New: Feverstone Wilds bison leg and ribs now smoke into cured red meat, and
    the cockatrice tail into cured poultry, one for one. They had no smoked or
    cured version of their own, so the smoker refused them.
  - Whole fish (base game and Feverstone Wilds) still can't be smoked. Fillet
    them first.

2.12.0  - New: Potbelly Stove and Smoker
  POTBELLY STOVE (Copper, Bronze, Iron, Steel)
  - A small black cast-iron potbelly stove with tier-metal trim, for cabins
    and corners. One cooking spot on the hot plate on top.
  - Built on the base game firepit: cooking pots, single items like a steak,
    serving meals from the pot, shift-adding fuel and lighting it all work as
    on a firepit, and so do other mods that cook on a firepit.
  - Cooks x1.0 / x1.1 / x1.25 / x1.4 as fast as a firepit. Fuel lasts
    x1.5 / x1.75 / x2.0 / x2.25 as long. Max heat 450 / 500 / 550 / 600 C.
  - Keeps a room warm like a firepit, with the fire's crackle.
  - Takes a stovepipe on its flue at the back, and gets the 25% chimney draft.
  - Recipe: 1 copper plate, 1 copper ingot and 5 fired bricks. Upgrade with a
    plate of the next metal on each side, like the other stoves.
  SMOKER (Copper, Bronze, Iron, Steel)
  - A 2-block farm smokehouse: plank walls on a stone footing, shingled gable
    roof with a vent, and an iron firebox at the base. The tier shows in the
    hinges, latch, ridge and firebox trim.
  - Hang raw meat or fish on the hooks (4 / 6 / 8 / 10) and keep a small fire
    going. The chamber stays at 60-120 C. Smoking takes about 24 / 20 / 16 / 12
    in-game hours. It pauses, not resets, if the fire goes out.
  - Wood gives full smoke. Charcoal and coal burn clean and smoke at a quarter
    of the speed.
  - Without Rustic Cookery, raw meat becomes the base game's cured meat.
  - With Rustic Cookery: meat slabs become smoked slabs, raw red meat and
    bushmeat become its smoked meat, and whole fish become its smoked fish.
  - Meat keeps twice as long while it hangs. Open the door to see it hanging.
  - Very smoky: it needs a chimney above it, or to be outdoors or in a room
    with a way out for the smoke.
  - Recipe: 5 planks, 2 stones and 1 copper plate. Upgrade with a plate of the
    next metal on each side.
  RUSTIC COOKERY
  - Braising, roux, fried and scrambled eggs work on the Potbelly Stove with
    tongs, as on a firepit (issue #1).
  - The clay oven exception from 2.11.0 stays for now. It will be removed once
    braising on the Potbelly Stove and Pot Roasts in the Range oven are tested.
  GENERAL
  - Closes issue #2 (small stove for cabins).

2.10.0  - First public release (ModDB)
  KITCHEN RANGE (Copper, Bronze, Iron, Steel)
  - 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 burner plates. Each takes a cooking pot, or a single item
    like a steak.
  - Built-in oven from Bronze up (1 / 2 / 3 slots). Bread comes out when it's
    done and never chars.
  - Cooks x1.1 faster than a firepit on Copper, up to x1.6 on Steel.
  - Shows what's cooking: stew in the pot, meat on the plate, bread on the
    oven rack.
  - Large Pots in Copper, Bronze and Iron hold 8 / 10 / 12 servings.
  KILN OVEN (Copper, Bronze, Iron, Steel)
  - Fires 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 raw clay items at once: moulds, pots and crucibles,
    spread out evenly inside the kiln.
  - Firing runs on the in-game clock and is quicker than a pit kiln: 12 / 10 /
    8 / 6 in-game hours, and about half that with charcoal or coal.
  - Crucibles use the same slots as pottery, so it fires and smelts at the same
    time. A crucible glows in the kiln once its metal is molten.
  - Higher tiers run hotter than their fuel: up to 1100 / 1475 / 1700 / 1950 C.
  - Fuel lasts 3 to 4 times longer than in an open fire.
  CHARCOAL RETORT (Copper, Bronze, Iron, Steel)
  - Firewood and logs make charcoal at twice the yield of a base game charcoal
    pit.
  - Black and brown coal make coke at the base game coke oven rates, on every
    tier.
  - Runs on the in-game clock, at about 1 in-game hour per charcoal.
  - Open the hatch to see the wood or the charcoal inside.
  - Gives off 2.5 times the smoke of the other stoves.
  SMOKE, CHIMNEYS AND STOVEPIPES
  - A fire in a sealed room with no chimney fills the room with smoke, and
    anyone inside starts choking. This also applies to the base game firepit
    and clay oven.
  - A Clay Brick Chimney above a stove, or joined to it by stovepipe, makes it
    work 25% faster.
  - Stovepipes join up on their own and sit flush against walls, roof and floor.
  - A chimney just above a roof one or two blocks thick still counts.
  GENERAL
  - Stoves face you when placed and turn with the wrench.
  - Doors open and close with the stove's window, with a plain metal hinge
    sound on the kiln, range and retort.
  - A torch or firestarter lights a stove without opening its window.
  - Stoves can be picked up with Carry On, if that mod is installed and its
    settings allow it.
  - The base game clay oven recipe is disabled, because the Kitchen Range
    replaces it.

2.8.1  - Kiln firing times balanced against the base game pit kiln
  - Firing now runs on the in-game clock (like the pit kiln), not real seconds.
  - Hours to fire a pot or mould at minimum heat: Copper 12, Bronze 10, Iron 8,
    Steel 6. Crucibles take 1.5x as long (same ratio as the base game).
  - Hotter fuel fires faster, like the pit kiln: charcoal or coal roughly
    halves the time (Copper about 6.5 h, Steel about 3.3 h). For comparison the
    base game pit kiln takes 20 h with firewood and 10 h with charcoal.
  - A chimney's 25% draft bonus still applies.
  - The kiln window now shows hours left, e.g. "Firing 40% (about 4h left)".
  - Crucible smelting is unchanged (still real-time, like the firepit).

2.8.0  - Tier rebalance from Adam's notes, coke fix, pipes on the floor
  - Kiln Oven, all tiers: reaches 1100 C and can smelt in crucibles (copper
    melts at 1084 C in the base game, the hottest crucible metal). Firing slots
    are now Copper 2, Bronze 3, Iron 4, Steel 5 (were 2/4/6/8). Needs charcoal
    or coal to get that hot.
  - Kitchen Range ovens: Bronze bakes at normal clay-oven speed, Iron a bit
    faster (x1.15), Steel faster again (x1.3). Burners unchanged.
  - Charcoal Retort, all tiers: max heat raised to 500/525/550/575 C so every
    tier reaches the 450 C coke needs. Coke runs at the same speed on every tier
    (60 s per 4 coal). Charcoal keeps its tier speed bonus.
  - Coke: any coal the base game can coke is accepted (black coal 4 -> 3 coke,
    brown coal 4 -> 2). Charcoal can no longer be put in the retort's input
    slot; if it's already there the window says charcoal can't become coke.
  - Stovepipes: a pipe running along the floor now sits on the floor, the same
    way pipes under a roof sit against the roof. Bends that turn up a wall from
    the floor line up with the run.

2.6.1  - Retort charcoal yield now twice the base game
  - Base game charcoal pits give about 5.5 charcoal per 32 firewood, so the
    retort now gives about double that (about 10.7 per 32).
  - Firewood: 3 firewood makes 1 charcoal (was 2).
  - Logs: 3 logs make 4 charcoal (was 1 for 1). A log is 4 firewood, so this is
    the same 3 to 1 rate.
  - Batch time and coke are unchanged.

2.6.0  - Coke in the Charcoal Retort
  - The Charcoal Retort can now make coke from coal, at the base game's coke
    oven yields: black coal gives 3 coke per 4 coal (75%), brown coal 1 coke per
    2 coal (50%). Anthracite is not coked, same as the base game.
  - Coke needs 450 C and takes 60 seconds a batch, so it needs a Bronze, Iron or
    Steel retort. A Copper retort tops out at 400 C and says so in its window.
  - Charcoal is unchanged: 2 firewood or 1 log per charcoal, 40 seconds a batch.
    (Base game charcoal pits give about 5.5 charcoal per 32 firewood, so the
    retort is roughly 3 times as generous.)
  - The retort window text now reads "Making coke" when it is working on coal.

2.4.0  - Crucible smelting in the Kiln Oven (Iron and Steel only)
  - Iron and Steel Kiln Ovens can smelt metal. Put a crucible in any firing
    slot (no separate crucible slots).
  - Crucibles and clay pots/moulds work together: mixed loads smelt and fire
    at the same time, each in its own firing slot.
  - Each firing slot has four ingredient slots beside it. They only accept
    items while a crucible is in that slot. Removing the crucible drops its
    ingredients.
  - Uses the game's own crucible rules: the highest melting point in the mix
    must be reached, then it runs for the combined melt time.
  - Status text per slot: Needs X C, Smelting N%, Add ingredients, Not a valid
    mix, Molten metal - pour into a mould.
  - The finished metal replaces the crucible in the same slot. Take it out and
    pour it into a mould as you would at a firepit.
  - Iron and Steel kiln window is one column of rows. Copper and Bronze keep
    the two-column layout. Copper and Bronze kilns cannot smelt (they only
    reach 1000 and 1100 C).
